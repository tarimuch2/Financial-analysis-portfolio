## 2. Strategic Business Model Analysis: Coeur Mining
**File:** `Business Model Analysis.docx`

### Objective
Authored a comprehensive strategic analysis of Coeur Mining to evaluate its business model, competitive moat, recent M&A activity, and positioning within the current macroeconomic environment.

### Key Highlights
* **Asset Quality & Scale:** Analyzed Coeur's position as a senior U.S.-based metals producer with a USD 20 billion market capitalization and diversified revenue across gold (72%), silver (20%), and copper (8%).
* **Strategic M&A:** Evaluated the strategic fit of the New Gold acquisition, highlighting the operational synergies of combining underground expertise with block caving technology.
* **Macroeconomic Leverage:** Assessed the transformative impact of USD 4,100 per ounce gold prices on the company's operating leverage, free cash flow generation, and accelerated debt repayment.
* **Risk Assessment:** Outlined critical risk factors, including post-merger integration challenges between SilverCrest and New Gold, stringent Canadian environmental regulations, and operational transition risks.
